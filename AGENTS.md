The most important rule: prefer simplicity and consistency across the project. When addressing the user, be concise.

- Read [README.md](README.md) when working with the user-facing introduction, installation, or usage.
- Read [docs/GLOSSARY.md](docs/GLOSSARY.md) when working with domain concepts. Use its canonical terms in code, documentation, and discussions; surface ambiguities or conflicts before changing definitions.
- Read [docs/ARCHITECTURE.md](docs/ARCHITECTURE.md) when working with system architecture or module boundaries.
- Before changing or reviewing files, consult [docs/rules/index.md](docs/rules/index.md) and read and follow the rules matching their file patterns.
